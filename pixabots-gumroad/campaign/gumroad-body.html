I made a tiny pixel character creator called Pixabots. It generates 10,752 unique combinations from four categories — eyes, heads, bodies, and tops — all bouncing on a little idle loop.

This pack is a curated 2,000 of them, ready to use. Free.

What's inside

  • 2,000 PNGs at 240px — chat avatars, UI, favicons
  • 2,000 PNGs at 480px — cards, hero spots
  • 2,000 PNGs at 960px — retina, print
  • 2,000 PNGs at 1920px — full HD, posters
  • 2,000 animated GIFs at 480px — the signature bounce
  • 2,000 animated WebPs at 480px — modern format, smaller than GIF

That’s 12,000 files. Around 550 MB unzipped.

Every file is named by its 4-character ID (like 7k3a.png). Paste any ID into pixabots.com and you can keep editing it in the browser — change colors, swap parts, export at any size.

Use them for

  • Avatars in your SaaS, app, or Slack workspace
  • Placeholder characters in design files
  • Game NPCs, stickers, merch
  • Twitch and YouTube channel art
  • Anything where “I need a character and I don’t want to draw one” is the problem

License

Free for personal and commercial use. Modify, recolor, composite, redistribute as part of your work. The one thing you can’t do is resell the images as a standalone pack — but if you build something with them, ship it.

Want more?

The full set of 10,752 is browsable at pixabots.com with a live editor, palette controls, animated GIFs, and a free API. No signup. No rate limit on the UI.

There’s also an SDK on npm (@pixabots/core, @pixabots/react) and a CLI (npx pixabots).

Everything is open source: github.com/pablostanley/pixabots.
